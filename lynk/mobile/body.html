Compare metro, bus, auto and cab by cost, time, reliability and convenience in a single place. Prototype for Delhi NCR.

Try this

  1. Pick “Dwarka Sec 21 → Connaught Place”, switch off Cab, then find routes.
  2. Flip between Cheapest, Fastest and Convenient.
  3. Tap “Why this score?” to see how it is worked out.
  4. Choose a route, start the journey and step through it.
  5. Accept the re-route when it pops up, then split the fare.
Sample data. Fares, times and station links are illustrative, not live. Only two pilot corridors are wired in.
